# Documentation

These are the pages of qisqa's documentation, in reading order. The
[README](../README.md) has what qisqa is and a quick start.

1. [Configuration](configuration.md): every key of the config file, its
   default, and whether a change to it applies without a restart.
2. [Data folder](data-folder.md): what qisqa keeps there, when a change is
   on disk, backups, restoring a copy with `-skip`, and what happens when
   the disk fails.
3. [Accounts and domains](accounts-and-domains.md): the admin commands for
   domains, aliases, accounts and blocked hosts.
4. [API](api.md): creating, editing and deleting links, shortening the URLs
   inside a text, the errors, and the other addresses qisqa serves.
5. [Links](links.md): how codes work and how long to make them, secured
   links, password links and text mode.
6. [Logs](logs.md): the service log, and the columns of the access, admin
   and error logs.
7. [Running qisqa](running.md): running it as a service under Supervisor,
   the health check, and small servers.
8. [Behind nginx](nginx.md): nginx in front of qisqa, for HTTPS.
9. [Docker](docker.md): the image and the compose file.
10. [Speed](speed.md): what qisqa did on a laptop and in a small container,
    and the store's own benchmarks.
11. [Development](development.md): the tests, the code, and how a release is
    made.
